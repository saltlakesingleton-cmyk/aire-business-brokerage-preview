OWNER QUESTIONS

Thinking about selling?

How much is my business worth?

Value is shaped by profitability, industry, recurring revenue, assets, risk, growth potential, and the strength of your financial records. A private review is the right place to start.

How long does it take to sell a business?

Timelines vary by industry, size, financial readiness, buyer financing, and deal complexity. A well-prepared business usually has a better path to a clean close.

Will my employees and customers find out?

Confidentiality is central to the process. Buyer information is controlled and serious prospects are qualified before sensitive details are released.

When should I begin planning an exit?

Earlier than most owners think. Even if a sale is years away, preparing financials, systems, and transition plans can create more options and increase value.
